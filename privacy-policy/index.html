Privacy policy

This notice explains how Clear Travel Cover handles personal information when you visit the website or contact us.

Last updated 15 September 2026Published by Justinas Spokas
Data controller

Justinas Spokas is the data controller for Clear Travel Cover. Contact: justinasspokas@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Website analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics may collect information about your browser, device, approximate location, pages visited and interactions. Our custom events can record use of the trip planner, including trip type, selected destination and traveller-count category. We do not intentionally send your name, email address or departure date to Google Analytics.

Cookie preference

We store your analytics choice locally in your browser so the site can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics.

Messages you send

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, name, company and message. Do not send medical records, payment card details or policy passwords.

Technical hosting data

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request and security information as part of providing its hosting service under its own privacy terms.

Why we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To understand aggregate website usage after consent.
  • To remember your cookie preference.
  • To respond to questions, corrections, partnership enquiries and privacy requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect legitimate rights.

Legal bases

We rely on consent for optional Google Analytics. You can withdraw consent through “Cookie settings” in the footer. We may rely on legitimate interests to operate and secure the site and respond to ordinary correspondence, or take steps at your request when discussing a potential service or partnership.

Who receives information

Service providers may process limited information for us, including Google for consented analytics, GitHub for website hosting and Google/Gmail for email communications. We do not sell personal information.

International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where required, providers use recognised transfer safeguards. Their own privacy notices explain their processing locations and safeguards.

How long we keep information

We keep correspondence only for as long as needed for the enquiry, legal obligations or legitimate record-keeping. Analytics retention follows the configured Google Analytics settings, and aggregated reports may remain after identifying data has been removed.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to processing, receive portable data and withdraw consent. Email your request to the controller. We may need to verify your identity.

Complaints

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. You may also contact the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

We will update this notice when the website, partners or data practices materially change.
