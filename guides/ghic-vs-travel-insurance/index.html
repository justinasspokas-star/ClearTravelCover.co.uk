GHIC vs travel insurance: what’s the difference?

They solve different problems. For many European trips, carrying both provides broader protection than relying on either one alone.

Reviewed 14 September 2026General information for UK travellers
Quick answer

GHIC provides access to eligible medically necessary state healthcare in participating countries. Travel insurance can provide wider financial protection for eligible emergencies, cancellation, baggage and repatriation, depending on the policy.

What exactly is a GHIC?

The UK Global Health Insurance Card demonstrates entitlement to certain state-provided healthcare during a temporary stay in participating countries. Treatment is provided on the same basis as for a locally insured person, so it may not always be free.

Apply only through the official NHS service. The legitimate card is free. Eligibility and coverage differ by destination, so check current government guidance before each trip.

What travel insurance adds

Travel insurance is a contract whose benefits depend on the policy wording. It may cover eligible emergency medical expenses, repatriation, cancellation, curtailment, baggage, personal liability and disruption. Each section has limits, conditions and exclusions.

Insurance can also consider parts of a trip that a health card never addresses: non-refundable accommodation, missed travel connections, belongings and planned activities.

Why medical repatriation matters

A GHIC does not pay to return you to the UK. If your medical condition requires a special flight, escort or air ambulance, the cost can be substantial. Review how the insurer defines medical necessity and who authorises transport.

Pre-existing conditions

GHIC may provide medically necessary state treatment for an existing or chronic condition in participating countries, but it is not designed for travelling specifically to obtain treatment. Travel insurers require accurate medical declarations and may apply additional terms or exclusions.

How to use them together

  1. Carry a valid GHIC or eligible EHIC where it applies.
  2. Purchase appropriate travel insurance for the complete trip.
  3. Declare medical conditions accurately.
  4. In an emergency, use local services and contact the insurer’s assistance team promptly.
  5. Keep documents and receipts according to the claim instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Is a GHIC free?

Yes, when obtained through the official NHS application service.

Does GHIC cover Spain?

It can provide access to medically necessary state healthcare in Spain, subject to eligibility and local arrangements.

Can an insurer require me to use GHIC?

Policy conditions differ. Read the medical claims section to understand how the insurer expects you to use available state healthcare.
