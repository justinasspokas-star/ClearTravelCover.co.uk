About Clear Travel Cover

Clear information for UK travellers

Clear Travel Cover is an independent digital publishing project designed to make travel insurance easier to understand.

Last updated 15 September 2026Published by Justinas Spokas
Who runs this website?

Clear Travel Cover is owned and published by Justinas Spokas. Questions about the website can be sent to justinasspokas@gmail.com.

Why Clear Travel Cover exists

Insurance wording can be difficult to compare. Limits, excesses, medical screening and activity exclusions are often more important than a headline price. This website was created to organise those questions into practical destination and traveller guides.

What we currently provide

At this stage, Clear Travel Cover publishes general educational information. We do not currently arrange, sell or recommend insurance products, provide personalised financial advice, collect payments or display live insurance quotations.

The comparison service shown on the home page is in development. If commercial insurance services are introduced, they will be provided through an appropriately authorised partner and the website’s regulatory disclosures will be updated before launch.

How content is prepared

Our guides use sources such as GOV.UK, the NHS, MoneyHelper, the FCA and destination-specific FCDO advice. Pages show a review date and link to supporting sources. Read our editorial policy for the full process.

Independence and commercial relationships

Clear Travel Cover may later earn commission from clearly identified affiliate links or commercial partners. Payment does not turn general information into a personal recommendation. We explain current and future relationships in our affiliate disclosure and will state which providers are included in any comparison.

Corrections and feedback

Travel and insurance information changes. If you spot something inaccurate, outdated or unclear, contact the publisher. We will check the relevant primary source and update the page when appropriate.
