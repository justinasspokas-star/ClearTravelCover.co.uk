UK to USA travel guide

Travel insurance for the USA

Medical care in the United States can be expensive, making policy limits, exclusions and emergency assistance particularly important to compare.

Reviewed 14 September 2026Published by Justinas SpokasEditorial policy
Quick answer

For a trip to the United States, look for a policy whose geographical area explicitly includes the USA and whose medical, repatriation and cancellation terms match your itinerary. The cheapest worldwide policy may exclude the USA.

Why USA cover needs closer attention

The FCDO advises travellers to have adequate travel health insurance and accessible funds for medical treatment and repatriation. Unlike many European trips, a UK GHIC does not provide routine reciprocal healthcare protection across the United States.

Policy wording matters because American healthcare providers may request payment information and insurers can require contact with their assistance team. Save the emergency number and understand what to do before arranging non-urgent treatment.

What to compare for an American trip

  • Medical expenses: review the overall limit and conditions for inpatient and outpatient treatment.
  • Medical repatriation: check arrangements for transport to the UK when medically necessary.
  • Cancellation: match the limit to flights, hotels, tours, theme parks and other prepaid bookings.
  • Geographical area: select worldwide cover that explicitly includes the USA.
  • Car-related exclusions: travel insurance is not a replacement for suitable vehicle insurance.
  • Sports and activities: add appropriate protection for skiing, hiking, water sports or adventure activities.

Single trip or annual worldwide insurance?

A single-trip policy can be straightforward for one American holiday. Annual cover may suit frequent travellers, but many products separate “worldwide excluding USA” from “worldwide including USA”. Check the maximum trip length, age limits and whether every planned country is included.

Pre-existing medical conditions

Declare conditions accurately during medical screening. This includes physical and mental health conditions when requested, recent treatment and conditions under investigation. A policy may exclude an undeclared condition or apply special terms, so read the confirmation carefully.

Trips with several states or countries

Tell the insurer about the full itinerary, including stopovers and side trips to Canada, Mexico or the Caribbean. If you are taking a cruise, confirm that cruise cover and every port are included. For road trips, calculate the full duration rather than only hotel nights.

Before travelling

  1. Read current FCDO advice for the USA and every additional destination.
  2. Confirm that the policy area includes the United States.
  3. Check medical, cancellation and baggage limits against your needs.
  4. Declare medical conditions and planned activities.
  5. Carry the insurer’s emergency assistance details.
  6. Keep receipts and documents needed if you make a claim.

Frequently asked questions

Is “worldwide” always enough for the USA?

No. Some worldwide policies exclude the USA, Canada and the Caribbean. Check the exact region shown on the schedule.

Does travel insurance pay every medical bill directly?

Processes vary. Contact the assistance provider quickly and follow its instructions; smaller eligible costs may sometimes be reclaimed later.

Does a policy cover rental-car damage?

Not automatically. Rental-car protection is separate unless the policy expressly includes it.
