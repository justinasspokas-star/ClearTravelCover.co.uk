How our guides are produced

Editorial policy

Our standards for producing clear, useful and appropriately sourced travel insurance information.

Last updated 15 September 2026Published by Justinas Spokas

Publisher and responsibility

Clear Travel Cover is published by Justinas Spokas, who is responsible for the website’s editorial process. The site does not claim that its publisher is an insurance adviser, medical professional or FCA-authorised intermediary.

Purpose of our content

Our guides are designed to help UK travellers understand questions to ask and policy features to compare. They provide general information, not personalised insurance, legal or medical advice.

Sources

We prioritise primary and authoritative sources, including GOV.UK, the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, the NHS, MoneyHelper, the Financial Conduct Authority and official regulator or insurer documents where relevant. External sources are linked near the information they support.

Writing and checking process

  1. Identify a specific question relevant to UK travellers.
  2. Review current authoritative sources.
  3. Draft an original explanation in plain English.
  4. Check factual statements, links and regulatory wording.
  5. Add a clear review date and internal links.
  6. Revisit the page when official guidance or the service changes.

Use of digital and AI tools

Digital tools, including AI-assisted drafting and editing, may be used to organise research, improve structure or identify technical issues. Published content is checked by the publisher against cited sources. We do not use automation as a substitute for verifying important claims.

Commercial independence

Commercial partnerships may influence which providers can be displayed, but paid relationships will be disclosed. We do not publish invented ratings, fabricated customer reviews or unsupported “best” claims.

Dates and updates

A “last updated” or “reviewed” date reflects a meaningful editorial review. Dates are not changed solely to make old content appear fresh. Travel advice can change quickly, so readers should always check the latest official destination advice before travelling.

Corrections

Send correction requests to justinasspokas@gmail.com with the page URL and the information you believe is wrong. Material corrections will be made as soon as reasonably possible after verification.
