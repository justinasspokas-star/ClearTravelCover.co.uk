Affiliate disclosure

We want visitors to understand when Clear Travel Cover may benefit financially from a link or purchase.

Last updated 15 September 2026Published by Justinas Spokas
Current position

Clear Travel Cover does not currently display live insurance offers or active comparison results. Commercial partnerships are being explored. This disclosure will be updated when partner links go live.

How affiliate relationships work

In the future, Clear Travel Cover may receive a commission when a visitor follows a partner link and purchases an insurance policy or related service. The partner tracks the referral and may pay us without the visitor paying Clear Travel Cover directly.

How links will be labelled

Commercial links or placements will be identified clearly using wording such as “Affiliate link”, “Ad” or an adjacent disclosure that a commission may be earned. We will not hide the commercial nature of a link only in this page.

How commissions can affect the site

Commercial agreements can influence which providers or services are available for inclusion. Clear Travel Cover may not cover the whole market. Our How we compare page explains how scope, ordering and sponsored placement will be disclosed before launch.

Editorial content

General guides are intended to help readers understand insurance concepts and trip-planning questions. An affiliate relationship does not make a product suitable for every reader and does not replace reading the provider’s policy wording.

No direct payment to Clear Travel Cover

Visitors should not send insurance premiums or payment-card details to Clear Travel Cover. Any future insurance purchase will take place through the identified insurer, broker or authorised comparison partner.

Questions
